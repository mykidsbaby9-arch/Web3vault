WEB3VAULT – COMPLETE WEB3 TRADING & ASSET MANAGEMENT PLATFORM

Build a fully responsive, production-ready Web3 platform called Web3Vault that combines wallet management, live trading, token swapping, bridging, rewards, airdrop claiming, portfolio tracking, NFT management, and advanced security tools.

PLATFORM VISION

Web3Vault should be a modern crypto super-app where users can connect their wallets, trade assets, manage portfolios, bridge tokens across networks, claim rewards, track NFTs, and access Web3 services from a single dashboard.

The design must feel premium, futuristic, secure, and similar to leading crypto platforms.
